HOW TO PLAY

  • HOLD LEFT-click (OR SPACEBAR) to DASH
  • RIGHT-click (or W) to shoot water
  • Press ENTER in-game to chat
  • HOLD W in a lake to dive underwater!
  • Eat players/food outlined LIGHT-GREEN!
  • Avoid players outlined RED (They can eat you)!
